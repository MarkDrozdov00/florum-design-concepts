Our Apartments

Studio

The Studio (29 sq m) is perfect for two to three guests, featuring a cozy sitting area, queen bed, kitchenette, and allergy-friendly furnishings for a comfortable extended stay.

Deluxe Studio

For larger groups, the Deluxe Studio (35 sq m) accommodates four to five guests, with a queen bed, sofa bed, and generous kitchen area, ideal for families or corporate groups.

Rose Studio

The Rose Studio (29 sq m) offers a romantic setting with a private terrace and stunning city views, perfect for couples or small groups wanting a memorable Vienna stay.

Why Vienna?

Vienna, known as the world's most livable city, offers a rich culture, green spaces, and superb public transit, making it the perfect place for long-term residents to experience both work and relaxation.

Located in the vibrant second district, Florum Hotel provides easy access to Vienna's historic and business areas, making it ideal for extended stays.
