Serviced Apartments

Advantages of Serviced Apartments

Each of our rooms is thoughtfully designed with apartment-style amenities, including a fully equipped kitchenette and stylish furnishings.

  • Home-like comfort
  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Cost-effective for longer stays
  • Hotel services
  • Flexible accommodation
  • Privacy and independence
  • Continental breakfast available for €15 per day

Why Vienna?

Vienna, known as the world's most livable city, offers a rich culture, green spaces, and superb public transit, making it the perfect place for long-term residents to experience both work and relaxation.

Located in the vibrant second district, Florum Hotel provides easy access to Vienna's historic and business areas, making it ideal for extended stays.

Pet-friendly stays at Hotel Florum

Travelling with your pet? Hotel Florum welcomes four-legged companions and offers a comfortable base for exploring Vienna together. Please let our team know before arrival so we can prepare for your stay.
