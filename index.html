Location & Conscious Stay

Central Vienna, easy arrivals, thoughtful comfort.

Located in Vienna's 2nd district, Florum Hotel offers easy access to the city centre, public transport, business areas and cultural highlights. Whether you are visiting for work, leisure or a longer stay, our location makes it simple to move around Vienna.

We also support a more conscious stay through practical measures such as reduced single-use amenities, waste separation and energy- and water-conscious daily operations.
