Sustainability

Our sustainability commitment

At Hotel Florum, we aim to reduce our environmental impact through practical, everyday choices across hospitality, energy, amenities, and operations.

Catering & Food Offer

Our menu includes vegetarian and vegan options to support more sustainable dining choices.

Waste Management

We use reusable packaging and apply careful waste separation to reduce landfill contributions.

Eco-Friendly Amenities

Shower gels, soaps, and shampoos are provided in dispensers to help minimize plastic waste.

Renewable Energy

We source our energy from a provider that uses green, renewable sources.

Water & Cleaning

Our measures include flow limiters, aerators, eco-friendly shower heads, dual-flush toilets, and environmentally friendly cleaning agents.
