Arriving by public transportation

The nearest stop is the U2 underground station Taborstraße, only 200 m or a 2-minute walk from reception. The U1/U4 station Schwedenplatz is almost as close at 800 m or a 10-minute walk.

Coming from the airport, railway station or bus terminal? The following routes will bring you safely to Hotel Florum.

From Vienna Airport

Reach Hotel Florum by City Airport Train (CAT), suburban railway or Railjet.

CAT

The fastest option takes you directly to Wien Mitte. Continue on U3 to Volkstheater, then change to U2 for Taborstraße. CAT tickets are available from airport ticket machines.

CAT ➜ Wien Mitte ➜ U3 Volkstheater ➜ U2 Taborstraße

S7

With the S7 suburban train, change only once at Praterstern and take U2 to Taborstraße.

S7 ➜ Praterstern ➜ U2 Taborstraße

Railjet

Take the Railjet to Vienna Central Station, continue on U1 to Karlsplatz and change to U2 for Taborstraße.

Railjet ➜ Wien Hauptbahnhof ➜ U1 Karlsplatz ➜ U2 Taborstraße

6 tips for your Vienna stay

Use Vienna’s excellent transport network

Vienna’s underground and tram network takes you quickly and easily throughout the city. Tickets are available from station machines or online through the free WienMobil app. The Vienna City Card includes public transport and discounts at many museums and is available online or at reception.

Parking correctly in Vienna

Most districts are paid short-stay parking zones. Parking vouchers are sold at tobacconists, petrol stations, post offices, Wiener Linien advance-sale points and ticket machines. In the 2nd district, residential streets are generally regulated Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 22:00; on main roads such as Taborstraße, from 8:00 to 18:00. The maximum stay is two hours.

Discover historic Taborstraße

First documented in 1406, Taborstraße is one of Vienna’s oldest streets. Today it runs 2.5 km from Vorgartenstraße to the Danube Canal. Notable buildings include the Church of the Brothers Hospitallers, the Odeon Theater and the Carmelite Church of St. Joseph.

Visit the Vienna Prater

The Giant Ferris Wheel in the Vienna Prater is one of the city’s best-known landmarks. The Prater is easy to reach from Hotel Florum and combines a traditional amusement park with a vast public green space for walks and sport.

Plan a market visit

Vienna’s classic market culture can be experienced at Karmelitermarkt in the heart of the 2nd district. Alongside fruit and vegetables, you will find international specialties, cafés, bars and restaurants—and it is only a short walk from Hotel Florum.

Discover green city oases

Beautiful Augarten is only a few minutes’ walk from the hotel. Its lawns, benches and level paths are ideal for relaxing, jogging or walking, while the two flak towers give the park its distinctive character.
