Rights under the GDPR

Under Article 13 GDPR, you have the following rights to ensure fair and transparent data processing:

You have a right of access under Article 15 GDPR to know whether we process data about you and, if so, to receive a copy of that data and information about the purpose of processing, the categories of data, recipients, transfers to third countries, storage duration, rights to rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, complaint options, the origin of the data if not collected from you, and whether automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

You have the right to erasure under Article 17 GDPR (“right to be forgotten”).
You have the right to restriction of processing under Article 18 GDPR.
You have the right to data portability under Article 20 GDPR.
You have the right to object under Article 21 GDPR.
If processing is based on Article 6(1)(e) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to processing. We will then examine whether we can legally comply with the objection.
If data is used for direct marketing, you may object to this processing at any time. We will then no longer use your data for direct marketing.

Under Article 22 GDPR, you may have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority, whose website is https://www.dsb.gv.at/.
